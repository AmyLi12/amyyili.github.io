About me

I love playing video games in my free time! Whenever I pick up a game, I can’t help but be enchanted by how the gameplay and design meld together to create immersive and seamless experiences. That blend of function and fun is what inspires me to design intuitive and playful interfaces for complex systems. As a designer, I’m always looking to grow through hands-on experience, collaboration, and lots of iteration!
