What is Le Goosery Store?

Le Goosery Store is a tower defense mobile game born out of Cornell University's GDIAC course, Advanced Topics in Computer Game Design. Over the course of a semester, I worked in a multidisciplinary team of ten people to bring this game to life. As the design lead and UI/UX designer, I directed the game's art direction and designed its UI while ensuring player satisfaction through playtesting sessions. By the end of the course, my team and I launched Le Goosery Store on both iOS and Android platforms, and presented it at Cornell's annual GDIAC Showcase.
